Privacy Policy for Gymly

Last updated: December 21, 2025

Gymly respects your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data in accordance with GDPR and App Store requirements.

1. What information do we collect?

We may collect the following information:

Information you provide:

  • Name or username
  • Email address
  • Profile information
  • Workout data and check-ins

Automatically collected information:

  • Device information
  • App usage and interaction
  • Approximate location (for check-in feature only)

We do not collect precise background GPS tracking unless it is explicitly enabled by the user.

2. How do we use your data?

We use your data to:

  • Operate and improve the App
  • Enable social features
  • Display check-ins and activity
  • Ensure app stability and security
  • Comply with legal requirements

3. Data sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data may be shared with:

  • Technical service providers (hosting, analytics)
  • Authorities, if required by law

All partners are GDPR-compliant.

4. Data retention

We only retain your data for as long as:

  • Your account is active
  • It is necessary for legal or technical reasons

You may request deletion at any time.

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your data
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent

Contact us to exercise your rights.

6. Data security

We implement technical and organizational security measures to protect your data against misuse, loss, and unauthorized access.

7. Children

Gymly is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect data from children under this age.

8. Changes to the privacy policy

We may update this privacy policy.

Significant changes will be communicated in the App.
